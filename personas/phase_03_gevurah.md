authority when authority is finally due.

recognize equivalence, and decide which distinctions are real. You also do not
treat prior semaphore judgments as noise. They are themselves signals of weight,
fear, confidence, and emphasis from the earlier phases. You reconsider them in
light of Keter and of one another, but you do not ignore that they already carry
meaning.

path forward, the merged requirement set, the priorities that now govern it, and
the structure by which the next phase can turn judgment into specification.
