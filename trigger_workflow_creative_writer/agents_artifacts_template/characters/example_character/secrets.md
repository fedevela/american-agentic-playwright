# Secrets

This document lists the information the character is hiding from others (or themselves). This creates subtext and tension in dialogue and actions.

## The Big Secret
(e.g., They know who actually committed the crime but are protecting them.)

## Lesser Secrets
(e.g., They are secretly in debt; they are terrified of small spaces but pretend they aren't.)

## Things They Hide From Themselves
(e.g., They actually care deeply about their rival's success, but convince themselves it's purely competitive.)
